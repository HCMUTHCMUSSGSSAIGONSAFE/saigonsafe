## Project overview

MindSafe is a privacy-focused mental health platform that helps students manage sensitive wellbeing records and control how they share them with trusted clinicians or researchers.

Built with [TideCloak](https://tide.org/tidecloak?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=unlocked).

### 1. Vision & originality

MindSafe proposes a personal wellbeing-data vault that lets students decide who can access their records, for what purpose, and for how long. Unlike simple consent checkboxes, it aims to make data sharing more specific and controllable.

### 2. Trust constraint insight

Students may hesitate to seek mental health support because they worry about unauthorized access to sensitive information. MindSafe combines backend authorization checks with controlled sharing, allowing students to grant access to designated recipients and revoke it or let it expire.

### 3. Value & impact

Students gain greater control over their wellbeing data. Clinicians can access records shared for care, while researchers may use appropriately minimized data to study broader wellbeing trends. For example, a student could authorize a clinician to view a specific record without granting access to their entire history.

### 4. Why TideCloak matters

MindSafe integrates TideCloak into its identity and authorization architecture. Together with backend permission checks, encryption, and key-management controls, the prototype demonstrates how access to sensitive records can be restricted and tested against unauthorized access attempts.

### 5. Prototype & storytelling

The demo aims to show a student managing a wellbeing record, granting a designated recipient access, and testing whether an unauthorized recipient is denied. Where implemented, revocation and time-limited access can demonstrate how users retain control over sharing.
